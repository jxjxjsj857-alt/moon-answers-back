The Moon finally answers back: **"Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 3 a.m. promises — and not one of you has ever wished me a good night back."** It speaks as the Moon, first person, answering all three at once — the wishes, the howls, the late-night promises. The voice is old and tired (four billion years of it), and the turn is the Moon wanting the one thing nobody ever gives it: someone wishing it a good night back. "Good night" is what you say to the Moon every night — and nobody has ever said it to the Moon itself.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It validates the line against the bounty gate (1–240 UTF-8 characters, not only white space, spoken as the Moon) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, reading the file back to prove the round trip. `--dry-run` validates and prints the line without writing; `ENTRY_OUTPUT_DIR` overrides the output directory for local testing.
